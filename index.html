How It Works

3 steps to lock in. Thats it.

1

Set Your Goals

Add goals for the day. Pick a category, set the time, and lock it in. You have 5 minutes to change your mind. After that? Its permanent.

→
2

Do The Work

Check off goals as you complete them. Watch your Chroma meter fill up. Build your streak. Flex on the leaderboard. Main character energy only.

→
3

Face The Music

Miss a goal? Choose your consequence: post a Reel, notify your parent, or take the solo L. Accountability is the whole point. No skipping.

Parent Mode

Parents can log in too.
No cap.

Your child sets a PIN. You enter your phone number + PIN. Boom — you see their goals, progress, and streak. No spying, no extras. Just the essentials. Its giving responsible parenting.

  • See today's goals and completion status
  • Track streak and overall progress
  • Know the accountability mode
  • No editing — read-only access
